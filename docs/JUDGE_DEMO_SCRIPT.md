# 3-minute CharterIQ judge demo

## 0:00 — The problem

“Imagine we need 300,000 MT of coal over six months from Australia to India's East Coast. The question is not only today's freight rate. We need to decide timing, vessel, port and contract coverage under uncertainty.”

## 0:25 — Analyze procurement

Enter:

- Coal
- 300,000 MT
- Australia
- Paradip
- 6 months

Click **Analyze procurement**.

Say:

“CharterIQ creates a shared scenario state. The freight signal is forecast first, but uncertainty is retained rather than hidden.”

## 0:55 — Vessel + port

Open Vessel Intelligence.

Say:

“A low rate does not help if the ship cannot physically fit the port. Hard LOA, beam and draft constraints are enforced before scoring.”

Open Port Feasibility.

Say:

“The system can also evaluate resilience across East Coast alternatives instead of treating destination as a fixed dropdown.”

## 1:35 — Contract coverage

Open Contract Coverage.

Say:

“We don't hard-code one universal best contract. We expose the trade-off between market exposure, flexibility, commitment and expected procurement cost.”

## 2:00 — What-if

Open What-if Lab.

Apply:

- Freight +15%
- Congestion +30%
- Vessel availability −35%

Say:

“This is the digital-twin moment: one shock changes the shared state, and the forecast, port pressure, vessel availability and contract landscape are recalculated together.”

## 2:40 — Differentiation

“Existing maritime platforms already provide strong chartering, market intelligence and scenario tools. Our focus is the predictive procurement layer for this specific East Coast bulk-cargo decision: future requirement → forecast → physical feasibility → coverage → stress test → explainable decision.”

## 2:55 — Close

“CharterIQ does not replace the charterer. It gives the charterer a decision surface before the commitment is made.”
