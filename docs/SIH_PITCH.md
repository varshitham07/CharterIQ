# SIH pitch — CharterIQ

## 20-second version

CharterIQ is a predictive bulk-cargo procurement digital twin. For a future cargo requirement, it combines freight forecasting with uncertainty, vessel-port feasibility, contract coverage analysis and disruption simulation so a charterer can evaluate decisions before committing in a volatile market.

## 60-second version

“Suppose a steel company needs 300,000 tonnes of coal over six months from Australia to India's East Coast. Today, the charterer may explore the market reactively and then separately check vessel fit, port limits and contract options. CharterIQ connects those decisions. It forecasts a freight range, checks vessel dimensions against port constraints, compares spot versus short-term and multi-voyage coverage, and lets the user stress the plan with freight spikes, congestion or vessel unavailability. Most importantly, the system explains why the decision landscape changes. We are not claiming to replace mature maritime systems; we are building a focused predictive procurement layer for this SIH problem.”

## Core line

**Don't only predict freight. Predict the consequences of procurement decisions.**
